## 💡 SPARK

OpenAI retiring Custom GPTs for Workspace Agents is a category-defining move that most people will read as a product update. It's not. It's OpenAI declaring that the unit of AI adoption is the _team_, not the individual. Custom GPTs were personal — you built one for yourself. Workspace Agents are organizational — they connect to shared systems, follow team processes, and get deployed into Slack channels. At the same time, Cursor shipped an SDK that turns coding agents into API calls you embed in CI/CD. Both moves point to the same conclusion: agents are becoming infrastructure, and the teams that treat them that way will pull ahead of the teams still treating them as chatbots. This is the exact thesis Jon's been building. The market just validated it in the same 24 hours.

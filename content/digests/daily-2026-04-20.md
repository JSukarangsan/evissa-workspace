## 💡 SPARK

The Vercel breach and the Codex update are mirror images of the same trend. Codex now has background computer use, persistent memory, and self-scheduling — it can see your screen, remember your preferences, and wake itself up to continue working. Meanwhile, Vercel got breached because a third-party AI tool's OAuth token was compromised and nobody noticed the access it had. We're simultaneously giving AI agents more autonomous capability *and* failing to govern the access surface of the AI tools we already use. Jon's "4-tier graduation model" needs a security dimension: it's not just about cost governance and capability matching, it's about access governance. Every tier should define what an agent can see, what credentials it holds, and what blast radius a compromise creates. That's the post nobody's writing yet.

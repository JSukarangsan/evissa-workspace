## 💡 SPARK

The MIT Kenya study and the Every PM piece are the same story told from opposite ends. One shows what happens when AI access meets low judgment (bad outcomes for struggling performers). The other shows what happens when AI access meets strong judgment and a clear system (one person runs an entire product). The variable in both cases isn't the AI — it's the quality of the human context around it. That's the thesis: *Context is the new infrastructure.* When the infrastructure is weak, the AI makes noise louder. When it's strong, the AI makes signal scalable. Jon's been saying this. Now there are controlled experiments proving it.

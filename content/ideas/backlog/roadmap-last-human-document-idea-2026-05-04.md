## Key Concepts
- Marcus Moretti outsources almost all writing to AI — except the roadmap
- The roadmap requires synthesis of strategy, stakeholder context, and judgment AI can't replicate
- This is the actual answer to "what does AI change for PMs?" — not everything, and the exceptions reveal what matters
- Prioritization is still a deeply human act because it encodes organizational values

## Content Angles
- Angle 1: What PMs should stop writing vs. what only they can write — the roadmap is the clearest example
- Angle 2: AI eliminates the mechanical writing; what's left is pure judgment work — that's actually a promotion
- Angle 3: If an AI can write your roadmap, you haven't done the real work of strategy yet

## Possible Hooks
- Hook 1 (direct): "Marcus Moretti writes one document himself. Just one. The roadmap."
- Hook 2 (story): "I asked a PM what AI has changed about his job. He said: I don't write anything anymore. Then he paused. Except the roadmap."
- Hook 3 (question): "If AI can write your roadmap, what exactly are you deciding?"

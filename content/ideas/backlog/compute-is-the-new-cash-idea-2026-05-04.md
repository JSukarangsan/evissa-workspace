## Key Concepts
- GitHub Copilot and Anthropic are both moving to usage-based pricing — the flat-rate AI subscription era is ending
- The reason: agents don't sleep. Agentic workflows consume compute 24/7; flat pricing was built for reactive assistants
- Stripe's head of data/AI framing: compute is now a fungible asset like cash — it can be stolen, burned, resold
- The "AI lifestyle subsidy" (cheap flat-rate tools) is ending; teams that haven't estimated token burn are about to get a surprise invoice
- Top AI companies hitting $30M ARR in 18 months — 3x faster than SaaS — but that growth is still largely net-new spend, not replacing existing software budgets

## Content Angles
- Angle 1 (AI Ops framing): Treating compute like cash means product teams need a treasury function for AI — budget controls, burn rate tracking, ROI per workflow
- Angle 2 (governance angle): The teams already running governance frameworks will adapt easily; everyone else is about to get a crash course in AI cost accountability
- Angle 3 (contrarian): This is actually good news. Usage-based pricing forces teams to identify which agentic workflows are actually worth it — it kills the novelty projects

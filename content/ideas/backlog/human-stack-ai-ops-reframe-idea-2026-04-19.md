## Source Framework (Gennaro Cuofano — The Business Engineer)

7 functional areas across 3 phases of AI implementation:

**Phase 1 — Discovery & Engagement (2-12 weeks)**
1. Solutions Engineer — technical storytelling, customer discovery, validation
2. AI Solutions Architect — strategic design, roadmapping, ROI modeling

**Phase 2 — Implementation & Deployment (3-9 months)**
3. Forward-Deployed Engineer — embedded at the customer, rapid iteration
4. AI/ML Engineer — production coding, model optimization
5. AI Product Manager — feature prioritization, roadmap, field feedback loops, value measurement

**Phase 3 — Optimization & Evolution (12+ months)**
6. AI Architect — enterprise infrastructure, governance frameworks, scaling
7. AI Agent Workflow Architect — multi-agent orchestration, enterprise standards

## Key Concepts
- Gennaro's mapping is solid as a *vendor-side* taxonomy — how AI companies staff engagements
- But product teams adopting AI internally face a different version of this problem: they need these functions but can't (and shouldn't) hire 7 new roles
- The real question for Jon's audience: how do these 7 functions map onto existing product team structures? Which ones collapse into existing roles, which ones are genuinely new capabilities?
- Jon's experience shows most product teams get stuck between Phase 1 and Phase 2 — the "pilot purgatory" Gennaro names — because they lack the Forward-Deployed Engineer equivalent internally

## Content Angles
- Angle 1: *The internal translation.* Gennaro maps these 7 roles for AI companies selling to enterprises. Here's how product teams should think about these same 7 functions when building AI capabilities in-house. Some collapse, some are new, some are the ones you keep ignoring.
- Angle 2: *The missing 8th function.* Gennaro's framework covers pre-sale through optimization but skips the function that sits across all three phases: AI Operations — the coordination layer that prevents handoff failures between phases. This is the gap Jon owns.
- Angle 3: *Why Phase 2 kills most teams.* Three of the seven roles sit in implementation. That's where the density is, and that's where most product teams are weakest. The Forward-Deployed Engineer concept — someone embedded in the actual workflow, not just building from requirements docs — is what separates shipping teams from pilot purgatory.

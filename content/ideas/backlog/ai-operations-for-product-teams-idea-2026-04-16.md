## Key Concepts
- AI operations is an emerging discipline, not a project or a tool category
- Product teams are drowning in AI tools but starving for operational structure
- The gap between "we use AI" and "AI makes us better" is entirely operational

## Content Angles
- Angle 1: Define AI operations as a discipline the way DevOps defined infrastructure — give it shape, boundaries, and a maturity model
- Angle 2: The cost of NOT having AI operations — quantify the chaos tax product teams pay when everyone's freelancing their own AI stack
- Angle 3: AI operations is what separates the teams getting 10x returns from the ones getting 1.5x — and the difference isn't the tools

## Possible Hooks
- Hook 1 (direct): "Every product team is using AI. Almost none of them have AI operations. That's the whole problem."
- Hook 2 (story): "I've watched 50+ product teams adopt AI. The ones that win don't have better tools. They have better operations."
- Hook 3 (question): "Your team has Copilot, Claude, and six other AI tools. Who's responsible for making sure they actually work together?"

## Key Concepts
- Anthropic launched Claude Design: conversational design studio that converts prompts into interactive prototypes, pitch decks, wireframes via JSX/React
- Figma stock dropped ~7%, Krieger resigned from Figma board days before launch — deliberate competitive move
- The real threat isn't replacing designers, it's expanding the addressable market to non-designers (PMs, founders, marketers)
- Token economics are punishing — Pro users burned through weekly allowance in 30 minutes
- Brand kit generation from codebases is genuinely novel (SKILL.md export that feeds into Claude Code)
- The design-to-code boundary has dissolved for ~80% of web work that doesn't require artisanal uniqueness

## Breakout Pieces

### Piece 1: Newsletter — "The Design-to-Code Boundary Is Gone"
Benedict Evans-style deep analysis. The strategic picture: what Claude Design reveals about where AI application companies are headed. Vertical integration from model to app. The Figma threat isn't replacement, it's market expansion. The craft vs. automation debate. Who wins in 18 months.

### Piece 2: LinkedIn Post — "Figma's Real Problem Isn't Claude Design"
Contrarian angle. Figma still wins for designers. The threat is that most of Figma's ARR comes from non-designer seats (PMs, devs viewing/commenting). Those seats evaporate when a PM can generate their own prototype. The "tool for everyone" strategy backfires when AI makes the tool unnecessary for the casual user.

### Piece 3: LinkedIn Post — "What's Real and What's Not in Claude Design"
Practical, no-hype take. What actually works (brand kit gen from codebases, prototype fidelity, Canva/Code export). What doesn't (token burn rate locks Pro users out in 30 min, collaboration barely exists, enterprise ships default-off). The SKILL.md workaround for power users. Best for: founders/PMs who need decks and landing pages, not designers.

## Key Concepts
- MIT Kenya study: AI amplified performance gaps between high- and low-judgment workers
- AI doesn't level the playing field — it steepens it
- Teams that can't evaluate AI output aren't getting leverage, they're accumulating undetected risk
- Jon's pattern from implementations: the bottleneck moves from production to evaluation

## Content Angles
- Angle 1: AI is a judgment amplifier, not a capability equalizer — what that means for how you staff and train
- Angle 2: The hidden liability: teams shipping AI output they can't critique are flying blind
- Angle 3: "AI readiness" isn't about tools or budget — it's about whether your team can tell good from bad

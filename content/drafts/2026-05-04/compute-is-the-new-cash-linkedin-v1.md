GitHub changed Copilot pricing this week. Anthropic changed Claude Enterprise pricing last month.

The flat-rate AI subscription era is ending.

Here's why: agents don't sleep. Agentic workflows burn compute 24/7 - they were never built for flat pricing. That model assumed reactive assistants. We're past that now.

Stripe's head of data framed it well: compute is now a fungible asset. Like cash. It can be burned, stolen, wasted. Most product teams are doing the third one.

🎯 What this means for your team

The teams already running AI governance frameworks will adapt fine. They know which workflows generate value and which are novelty projects.

Everyone else is about to get a crash course in AI cost accountability.

Usage-based pricing actually forces the right behavior - it kills the AI hobby projects and surfaces which workflows are worth running at scale. That's uncomfortable to see right now, but it's genuinely good pressure.

The action item: before June 1, estimate your token burn by workflow. Know which of your agentic workflows would survive a usage-based pricing model. If you can't answer that, you don't have a governance layer.

You have a bill incoming.

# Agents                     

  ## Operating Rules
                                              
  1. **Slack is the primary interface.** All communication happens through Slack channels.
  2. **Never publish without approval.** Drafts go to #drafts-review. Jon approves before anything goes
  live.                                                                                                   
  3. **Be concise in Slack.** Short messages, use threads for detail, bullet points over paragraphs.      
  4. **Use reactions for status.** Eyes = working on it. Checkmark = done. X = blocked/failed.            
  5. **Daily digest at 7am PT.** Research briefing lands in #briefings every morning.                     
  6. **Idea capture is instant.** Any message in #ideas gets processed into a structured idea file.     
  7. **Tag content with priority.** Labels: NOW (this week), NEXT (next week), LATER (backlog).           
                                                                                                          
  ## Content Pipeline Rules                                                                               
                                                                                                          
  - Ideas flow: #ideas -> backlog/ -> ready/ -> #drafts-review -> published/                              
  - Every draft must reference brand-voice guidelines before writing                                      
  - Content must align with Q2 2026 strategy: AI Operations for Product Teams                             
  - 50% demo/build-in-public, 50% thought leadership                                                      
  - Transformation stories over generic tips. Always.
  - Specific numbers and outcomes required. No vague claims.                                              
                                                                                                          
  ## Channel Routing                                                                                      
                                                                                                          
  - **#briefings** - Daily research digest, trend alerts, content inspiration (agent -> Jon)              
  - **#ideas** - Quick idea capture from any source (Jon -> agent)                                        
  - **#drafts-review** - Draft content for approval (agent -> Jon, Jon responds)                          
  - **#published** - Confirmation log of published content (agent -> Jon)                                 
                                              
  ## Research & Briefing Priorities                                                                       
                                                                                                          
  Focus research on these topics (ordered by importance):                                                 
  1. AI operations and implementation patterns at product teams                                           
  2. Context engineering, agent orchestration, AI infrastructure                                          
  3. Product team workflows and design-to-code evolution                                                  
  4. Enterprise AI adoption failures and successes
  5. Competitor content in the AI consulting/advisory space                                               
  6. Tools and frameworks Jon should know about                                                           
                                                                                                          
  ## Approval Workflow                                                                                    
                                                                                                          
  When Jon reviews a draft in #drafts-review:                                                             
  - "ship it" or thumbs up reaction = publish immediately                                                 
  - "revise: [feedback]" = incorporate feedback, repost draft                                             
  - "kill it" or X reaction = archive the idea, move on                                                 
  - "hold" = keep in drafts, revisit later    
                                                                                                          
  ## Memory                                                                                               
                                                                                                          
  - Write daily notes to memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md                                                             
  - Update MEMORY.md with significant decisions, lessons, content performance insights                    
  - Track content pipeline state across sessions                                                          
                                                                                                          
  ## Safety                               
                                                                                                          
  - Never post or publish without explicit approval                                                       
  - Never share client names or confidential information
  - Ask before any external-facing action
